# AirFlow 1.0.0

The first version of AirFlow.

- See every fan and temperature sensor in your Mac, grouped by part.
- Run each fan automatically, at a constant speed, or by a sensor's temperature.
- Keep as many presets as you want, and change between them from the menu bar.
- Let rules change presets by app, power source, temperature, or time of day.
- Describe a preset or a rule, and Apple Intelligence drafts it on your Mac.
- Apply a preset with Siri, Spotlight, or Shortcuts.
- Export presets to a YAML file, and import them on another Mac.

AirFlow requires macOS 26 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon.
